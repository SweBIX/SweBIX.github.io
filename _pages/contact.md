SweBIX is not a formal organization; this website is maintained by the owners of the [SweBIX GitHub organization](https://github.com/SweBIX).

## Get in touch

- [Join us](/#join-us)
- **Members:** reach everyone in our [Zulip channel](https://imagesc.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/658147-SweBIX).
- **Questions or suggestions:** [open an issue](https://github.com/SweBIX/SweBIX.github.io/issues) on GitHub.

## Privacy

We publish what members add to their member files in our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/SweBIX/SweBIX.github.io), plus their GitHub avatar if they provide a GitHub handle but no photo. Members consent to this by submitting their file (see the [member template](https://github.com/SweBIX/SweBIX.github.io/blob/main/_members/_template.md)). To change or remove your information, edit or delete your file in a pull request, respectively; earlier versions remain in the repository's history.

This website is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), sets no cookies of its own, and uses no analytics. It loads icons from jsDelivr, avatars from GitHub, and the calendar from Google, which may process your IP address and set cookies under their own policies. Our Zulip channel is hosted on the image.sc Zulip.
