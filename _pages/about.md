We are an open and welcoming do-ocracy\* dedicated to advancing computational aspects of bioimaging within Sweden and beyond. We enjoy working together, exchange knowledge, and coordinate our activities. Together, we get things done.

\* In a do-ocracy, whoever takes something on gets to shape it.
{: .small}

## Why SweBIX?

Bioimage computing in Sweden happens in many places: in research groups and core facilities at universities, at [SciLifeLab](https://www.scilifelab.se/), [NBIS](https://nbis.se/), [NMI](https://www.nmisweden.se/), and the [Sweden AI Factory](https://swedenaifactory.se/), at the [Data Centre](https://www.scilifelab.se/contact/data-centre/) and other domain-specific IT infrastructure providers, in companies, and more. Each of these has its own mandate and community, so people doing similar work often sit in different organizations without knowing of each other or their work.

SweBIX connects them across these boundaries. We are not a formal organization and do not replace any of the existing ones: SweBIX is a loose, voluntary network of people, independent of where they work. Everyone in bioimage computing is welcome, whatever their affiliation.

## Why the X?

The X stands for:

- **Experts**, with their expertise and experience
- **Exchange** and coordination of efforts, as an inclusive, non-hierarchical nexus for learning and collaboration
- **BIX ⊃ BIAS** (as in [GloBIAS](https://www.globias.org)): not only bioimage analysts, but everyone in bioimage computing: analysts, data stewards, IT infrastructure specialists, tool developers & RSEs, ...
