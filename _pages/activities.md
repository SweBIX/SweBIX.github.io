## SweBIX Fika

*First Tuesday of the month (except national holidays), 10:00, online, max. one hour, for members ([join us](/#join-us))*

We get together to catch up, learn from each other, discuss ongoing work, and align our efforts. After welcoming new members and sharing news and opportunities, we go around the virtual table: what has everyone been up to, what's next, and where could we team up? If someone has something to show, there's room for that too. Any remaining time is yours for questions, ideas, and feedback.

No slides, no recording, no minutes; just bring your own fika.

## SweBIX on Zulip

*Anytime, online, for members*

Between fikas, the conversation continues in our [private channel](https://imagesc.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/658147-SweBIX) on the [image.sc Zulip](https://imagesc.zulipchat.com): ask questions, share what you've found, and pick up where the last fika left off. It's also where you'll find the agenda and link for the next one. Once you've [joined us](/#join-us), we'll add you to the channel.
