## SweBIX Fika

*First Tuesday of the month (except national holidays), 10:00, online, max. one hour, for members ([join us](/#join-us))*

We get together to catch up, learn from each other, discuss ongoing work, and align our efforts. After welcoming new members and sharing news and opportunities, we pick up on the updates posted in the fika's Zulip topic: anyone with an update or an idea for teaming up gets the floor. If someone has something to show, there's room for that too. Any remaining time is yours for questions, ideas, and feedback.

No slides, no recording, no minutes; just bring your own fika.

## SweBIX on Zulip

*Anytime, online, for members*

Between fikas, the conversation continues in our [Zulip channel](https://imagesc.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/658147-SweBIX): ask questions, share what you've found, and pick up where the last fika left off. It's also where each fika gets its own topic, with the agenda, the link, and members' updates beforehand. Once you've [joined us](/#join-us), we'll add you to the channel.
