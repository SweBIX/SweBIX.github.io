{%- comment -%} Renders a member's avatar: their photo, otherwise their GitHub avatar, otherwise their initials. The initials are always rendered underneath, so they also show if an image fails to load. Parameters: member: member document variant: "small" (default) or "large" {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign member = include.member -%} {%- assign avatar_src = nil -%} {%- if member.photo -%} {%- assign avatar_src = "/assets/images/members/" | append: member.photo | relative_url -%} {%- elsif member.github -%} {%- assign avatar_src = "https://github.com/" | append: member.github | append: ".png?size=320" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- capture initials -%}{{ member.first_name | slice: 0 }}{{ member.last_name | slice: 0 }}{%- endcapture -%} {{- initials | upcase -}} {%- if avatar_src %}{% endif -%}
